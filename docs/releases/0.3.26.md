## Changed

- **Dictation keeps your last word.** Volant now keeps listening for a moment after you release the dictation key, so a final word spoken as you let go isn't cut off. (#327)
- **Dictation hears you from the start.** The microphone starts before the recognizer finishes getting ready, and what you say meanwhile is kept, so words spoken right as you press the key aren't lost. (#327)
- **An accidental tap types nothing.** A dictation that stays near silence is discarded without being transcribed. (#327)
- **AI Chat's helper does a little less work per message.** It no longer re-encodes tool updates to measure them or re-reads the agent's capabilities on every prompt. (#326)

Requires macOS 15 or later. Apple silicon and Intel. Dictation requires macOS 26.
