## Changed

- **Calculator answers follow your Mac's language and region.** Times use your Mac's 12- or 24-hour clock, and durations, counts and date answers use your language's words and number format, so a German Mac shows `21:00 CEST` and `2 Stunden 25 Minuten`. Copy copies what the card shows. Values meant for code — hex and RGB colors, ISO dates, Unix time, number bases — stay as they are, and a swapped query is written so Volant can read it back. English answers are unchanged. Translated words are unreviewed betas. (#334)
- **Dictation pastes on any keyboard layout.** When dictation types at the cursor, Volant now presses the key that types "v" in your layout, so it pastes on Dvorak instead of sending Command-K. U.S., AZERTY and "Dvorak – QWERTY ⌘" layouts work as before. (#333)

Requires macOS 15 or later. Apple silicon and Intel. Dictation requires macOS 26.
