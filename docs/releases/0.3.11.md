## New

- **Dictation vocabulary.** Teach dictation the names and technical terms it gets wrong, in Settings → Dictation → Vocabulary. Add a word such as Kubernetes, with anything it's heard as instead, and dictation writes it your way. Import a list from a text file, one word per line or heard and written pairs. Your list syncs between your Macs when iCloud settings sync is on. (#272)
- **Close matches.** Dictation also fixes words that sound like one of yours, so "Kubernitz" becomes Kubernetes without listing it. It only changes words the spell checker doesn't know; turn it off with Also fix close matches. (#273)

Requires macOS 15 or later. Apple silicon and Intel. Dictation requires macOS 26.
