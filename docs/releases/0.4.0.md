## New

- **Focus.** Type `focus` to start a session with a goal, a length and a block list of apps, websites and ready-made categories such as Social Media, News and Messaging. Blocked apps are hidden the moment they come forward, never quit, and a reminder shows your goal with Snooze for just that app or site. Volant remembers your setup, so `focus 25m <goal>` or a Toggle Focus Session shortcut starts the next one at once. (#390, #391)
- **Website blocking** in Safari, Google Chrome, Brave, Brave Origin and Vivaldi. When the browser in front shows a blocked site, Volant swaps the tab for a blank page and puts it back when you snooze, pause or end the session. macOS asks once per browser. Addresses are checked on your Mac and never saved. Firefox isn't supported. (#396)
- **Focus around the notch.** On a MacBook, the session sits around the camera notch and expands into its controls when you rest the pointer on it; reminders drop out of the notch. Other screens show a floating bar, and the menu bar is a third choice. (#395)
- **Breaks.** Turn on Breaks in Settings → General to be reminded to step away, after 45 minutes of active or clock time by default, with a heads-up first. Choose Gentle, Standard or Strict, and breaks wait while your camera or microphone is in use. Off unless you turn it on. (#394)

## Fixed

- Apps in the Applications folder in your home folder now show up in the launcher, such as apps installed by JetBrains Toolbox and Chrome or Brave web apps. (#393)
- File results show paths in your home folder as `~/…`. (#393)

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Dictation requires macOS 26.
