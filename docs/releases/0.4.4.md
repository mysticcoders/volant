## New

- **Let AI apps use Volant.** Turn on Connected Apps in Settings › AI, and Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Codex, Cursor and other apps that support MCP can search and read your Volant notes, list your snippets and quicklinks, use Volant's calculator, and check Breaks and Focus. With their own switches, they can also create notes, add to a note, and add snippets and quicklinks. Connect an app shows the line to copy for each app. It's off by default and stays on your Mac: AI apps start a small bridge inside Volant, Volant answers only that bridge, and nothing listens on the network. If Volant isn't running, or quits mid-session, the bridge starts it again. (#424, #425)
- **Updates show up sooner.** Volant now checks for updates every four hours instead of once a day, so a new version reaches the launcher's Update Volant row the same day. This starts once 0.4.4 is installed. (#423)

## Also

- Break alerts on your iPhone and Apple Watch, from 0.4.3, now work with the public Volant for iPhone beta on TestFlight.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Dictation requires macOS 26.
