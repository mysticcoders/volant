## New

- **Dictation typed at the cursor.** Choose **Type at the cursor** in Settings → General → Dictation, and what you say is typed into the app you are using, phrase by phrase while you speak or all at once when you finish. Return finishes and Escape cancels. It uses a separate helper that runs only after you choose this option and asks macOS once for Accessibility, listed as Volant. It never reads the text of the field it types into, refuses password fields and secure input, and keeps your clipboard. **Copy to clipboard** stays the default and needs no permission. Requires macOS 26 or later, like dictation itself. (#207)
- **Several AI conversations at once.** **New** in AI Chat starts another conversation while the first keeps working. Switch between up to six with the buttons under the header; a raised hand marks one waiting for your permission. Contributed by @jrobgood. (#197)
- **An account per conversation.** If you use more than one Claude Code or Codex login, add each as an account in AI Settings. New conversations run under the account you choose and show its label. Volant passes only the account folder's path to the provider and never reads its contents. Contributed by @jrobgood. (#200)

Requires macOS 15 or later. Apple silicon and Intel.
