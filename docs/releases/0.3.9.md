## New

- **Filters inside extensions.** An extension can offer a dropdown at the end of the search field, such as Top, New, Ask and Show for Hacker News. Choose one, or press Command-P for the next, and the list updates right away. Volant remembers each extension's choice until it quits. Update Hacker News from the catalog to get its filters. (#260)

Requires macOS 15 or later. Apple silicon and Intel. Dictation requires macOS 26.
