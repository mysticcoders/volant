## New

- **Keyboard in the Rex Overview.** Use ← and → to move between tabs, ↑ and ↓ between the panes of a tab, Tab to jump to the next pane waiting on you, and Return to switch Rex to it. Esc closes the overview. (#284)
- **Floating and zoomed panes.** The overview draws floating panes where they sit, over the tiles, and shows a zoomed tab the way Rex does, with chips for the panes the zoom hides so nothing waiting is out of sight. (#285)
- **Minimize sessions.** Click the chevron beside a session's name, or press Space, to shrink it to a line of tabs with a dot per pane. It stays minimized on this Mac until you expand it. (#285)

Requires macOS 15 or later. Apple silicon and Intel. Dictation requires macOS 26.
