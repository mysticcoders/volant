## New

- **Extension Catalog in the launcher.** Type `catalog` or choose Extension Catalog to search reviewed extensions, read what each one does and which sites it reads, and install, update or turn one on without opening Settings. (#224, #225, #230)
- **Pin, hide and read state for Hacker News and Lobsters.** Press Command-K on a story to pin it, hide it or hide every story from its site; stories you open show as read. Update the readers from the catalog to get this. (#226, #227)
- **Extensions can remember things on this Mac.** An extension that asks to "Remember settings and lists on this Mac" keeps a small amount of data here, never synced or backed up. Settings → Extensions has Clear Data, and uninstalling removes it. (#226)
- **Extension icons.** Extensions can show their own icon in the launcher and Settings; Hacker News and Lobsters use their sites' icons. (#222)
- **Cleaner dictation.** Filler words like "um" and "uh" are removed from English dictation as you speak. Turn it off in Settings → General → Dictation. (#229)
- **Dictation sounds.** A short sound plays when dictation starts listening and another when it stops. (#229)

## Changed

- Extensions that are off no longer appear in everyday search; find them with `ext` and a name, or in the catalog. (#230)
- Settings → Extensions has one Refresh, which reloads installed extensions and checks the catalog. (#230)

Requires macOS 15 or later. Apple silicon and Intel. Dictation requires macOS 26.
