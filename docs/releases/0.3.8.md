## New

- **Badges on extension rows.** Rows can show up to three small badges, such as points, comments, site, author or time, instead of packing them into the subtitle. Update Hacker News from the catalog to see points and comments at a glance. (#258)
- **Row links in Actions.** Command-K on an extension row lists Open Link, its second link (such as Open Comments) and Copy Link, alongside the extension's own actions. (#258)

Requires macOS 15 or later. Apple silicon and Intel. Dictation requires macOS 26.
