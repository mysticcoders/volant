## New

- **Extensions get their own space in the launcher.** Choose a list extension, or type its keyword and press Return, to enter it: a chip with its name sits at the start of the search field, and what you type goes to the extension as you type, not to the launcher's search. Text after the keyword comes with you, so `hn ask` enters Hacker News searching for "ask". Escape clears the text; a second Escape, Delete in an empty field or clicking the chip goes back. Extensions that search, such as Wikipedia and npm, wait for you to type. (#256)

Requires macOS 15 or later. Apple silicon and Intel. Dictation requires macOS 26.
