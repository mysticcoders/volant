## Changed

- **Community extensions explained before you turn them on.** With community extensions off, Settings → Extensions now explains how Volant keeps them in check (sandboxed, reviewed and signed, you approve access, source you can read) and offers one Turn On button; the catalog and community extensions stay hidden until then. When they're on, a bar at the top offers Turn Off. Extension rows also keep their description readable at the smallest window size. (#252)

## Fixed

- In Notes, a shortcut pressed just as Actions or Browse opened could still run, such as Command-E leaving editing or Command-Delete trashing the note behind it. (#254)

Requires macOS 15 or later. Apple silicon and Intel. Dictation requires macOS 26.
