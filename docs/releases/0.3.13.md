## New

- **Rex Overview.** Choose Open Rex Overview, the first item when you type `rex`, or type `rex overview`, to see every Rex session in its own window. Each tab is drawn as its panes are laid out in Rex, colored by status, with what's waiting counted per tab and session. Click a pane to switch Rex to it. (#279)
- **Pane previews.** The overview shows the last 12 lines of each pane's screen. They're off until you turn them on, in the overview or in Settings → Status Bar → Pane previews in Rex Overview. Volant reads screens only while the overview is open, keeps those lines in memory and never saves, logs or sends them. The choice is per Mac and isn't synced. (#279)

Switching Rex to a pane in another session still needs Remote Control turned on in Rex Settings → Rex Server.

Requires macOS 15 or later. Apple silicon and Intel. Dictation requires macOS 26.
