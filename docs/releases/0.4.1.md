## New

- **Weather.** Type `weather` for a forecast card where you are, or `weather tokyo` (also `weather in tokyo`) for anywhere else. The card shows the current temperature and conditions, today's high and low, the next hours and the week; Tab switches between them and Return opens the Weather app. Forecasts come from Apple Weather and appear once you pause typing. Your location is rounded to about 11 km, asked for the first time you use it, and never saved. (#408)
- **More units.** Tonnes, short and long tons, micrometers (`µm`, `um`, microns) and nanometers convert, as do tebibytes, petabytes and pebibytes, with long names such as `512 mebibytes in mb`. Swap reads every new unit back the right way. (#397, #404)

## Fixed

- Apps in the Applications folder in your home folder, such as those installed by JetBrains Toolbox and Chrome or Brave web apps, now appear in the installed app. The 0.4.0 fix worked only in development builds. (#403)

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Dictation requires macOS 26.
