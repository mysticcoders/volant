## New

- **Notes in iCloud.** Turn on **Sync settings and notes with iCloud** in Settings → Data & Configuration and your notes move to iCloud Drive › Volant › Notes, where they stay plain Markdown files and follow you between your Macs. Your Mac's notes folder is kept as a dated backup, and turning sync off copies your notes back to this Mac. Sync stays off unless you turn it on. (#380)
- **Notes that are still downloading** from iCloud show as Downloading… and open once they arrive, instead of holding up the notes window. (#380)

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Dictation requires macOS 26.
