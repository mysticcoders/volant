## New

- **See your breaks in Settings › Your Volant.** Volant now keeps track of your breaks: how many you took each day, how many you skipped and how long they lasted, with today's numbers and a bar for each of the last seven days. Time away from your Mac that lasts a whole break counts as one. Break history stays on your Mac for 90 days, and Clear Activity History clears it. (#417)
- **Break alerts on your iPhone and Apple Watch.** Turn on Alert my iPhone and Apple Watch in Settings › General › Breaks, and Volant for iPhone tells you when a break starts and when it's over, through your own iCloud. Your watch shows them too, so you know when to come back. Only those two moments leave your Mac, never your break history or settings. It's off by default, and it needs Break Alerts in Volant for iPhone, which arrives in the next beta build on TestFlight. (#418, #419)

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Dictation requires macOS 26.
