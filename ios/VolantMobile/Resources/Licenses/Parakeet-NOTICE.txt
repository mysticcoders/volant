Parakeet Ultra speech model

Volant can download and use Parakeet Ultra for dictation:

- Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 by NVIDIA (https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3)
- Retrained as Parakeet Ultra by Moondream (https://huggingface.co/moondream/parakeet-ultra)
- Converted to Core ML by FluidInference (https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/parakeet-ultra-coreml)

Volant does not ship the model. It downloads FluidInference's files unchanged, the first time the
owner turns on Parakeet in Settings, and runs them on the device. The weights are provided as-is,
without warranties, as the license states.
